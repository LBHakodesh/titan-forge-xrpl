The problem

Thin markets lose trades.

When a payment on the XRP Ledger needs to cross from one currency to another, it needs offers waiting on the other side. Where the order book is thin, there is no path, and the payment simply fails.

133 cross-currency payments failed vs 79 succeeded. The top 5 providers supplied 97% of liquidity.

The engine

Six liquidity rings around every market.

Titan Ghost places offers in six rings around the market price. The inner rings quote tight, so everyday payments clear. The outer rings hold deeper liquidity further out, so larger payments still find a path.

  • VelocityInner · tight
  • BridgeRing 2
  • EarnRing 3
  • MoneyRing 4
  • DeepRing 5
  • CeilingOuter · deep
Existing GYW1313 technology

The XRP Ledger does the routing. Its built-in path-finding sends payments through Titan Ghost offers on its own, including XRP auto-bridging, where a payment hops from one currency through XRP into another.

New this weekend

What we built this weekend.

Built during the XRPL Commons AI Hackathon in London: two capabilities inside Titan Ghost, and one neutral protocol open to any liquidity provider.

Lending

Titan Ghost Liquidity Bootstrap (TGLB)Funds new markets in three tiers

TGLB gives a new market the liquidity it needs from day one. It is supported by a separate platform, Titan Ghost Liquidity Wake (TGLW).

  • TGLW is its own self-custodial liquidity platform. It supplies Tiers 1–2 from participants' own wallets.
  • Tier 3 is underwritten loans.
  • Two distinct platforms, linked: TGLW supports TGLB.

Agentic payments

TG MerchantAI agents buy market data over x402

AI agents can pay for Titan Ghost market data with x402 payments on the XRP Ledger, in the same flow they already use to request it.

  • Every payment needs a person's approval. Agents can ask; people decide.
  • Payments settle on the XRP Ledger.

Neutral protocol

XDLCXRPL Dynamic Liquidity Corridors

A neutral liquidity corridor for any XRP Ledger pair that has an AMM. It quotes at least 1 basis point better than the AMM price, fee included, at every depth. Providers' funds stay in their own wallets, longer commitments fill first, and there is no price oracle.

  • Quotes better than the AMM at every depth
  • Open to any provider: Titan Ghost, TGLW participants, other firms
  • Exit early, pay the providers who stay
Neutral protocol · Draft amendment, not yet submitted
