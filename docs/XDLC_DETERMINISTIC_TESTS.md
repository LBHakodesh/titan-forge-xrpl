# XDLC deterministic test vectors

Companion to `XDLC_AMENDMENT_PROPOSAL.md` (XLS-XXXX, unassigned; Draft-Local, not submitted).

**What this is:** vectors for the **executable specification model** `lib/xdlc-spec/model.mjs` (v0.2), a pure
JavaScript `apply(state, tx) -> { result, state, trace }`. **It is not xrpld code.** Passing shows the candidate rules
are internally consistent and keep the invariants; it says nothing about an xrpld implementation, which does not exist.

Run: `node --test test/xdlc-spec.test.mjs` (no network). Regenerate tables and `lib/xdlc-spec/summary.json` with
`node lib/xdlc-spec/render.mjs`.

## Fixture

`fixture()` in `lib/xdlc-spec/vectors.mjs`. Close time 1000, ledger 1; base fee 10 drops; reserve 1 XRP + 0.2 XRP per
object (Mainnet, 2026-10-25). XRP in drops, `USD.rIssuer` in micro-units. AMM USD/XRP: 10,000 USD / 20,000 XRP,
TradingFee 300 (0.3%), single LP `rLP`. Providers rAlice, rBob, rDave (USD), rCarol (XRP); taker rTaker; CLOB maker
rMaker; plus rFrozen, rNoLine, rPoor, rOutsider, rIssuer. Permissioned domain members: rAlice, rBob, rCarol.

Default corridor `CORRIDOR:rOwner:1`: USD/XRP, ImprovementBps 10, PauseBps 500, MinCommit 1 USD / 1 XRP,
MaxParticipants 8, gate 2 participants + 100 USD, MaturityTime 100000. Alice: 100 USD, LockDuration 10000, ExitFeeBps
500, MaxPerLevel 20, MinRate 1 XRP/USD. Bob: 100 USD flexible, MaxPerLevel 20. `ACTIVE` = create, Alice, Bob, advance
(activated t=1000).

## Vectors

`Fee` and `Sequence` are filled automatically. `tec` results claim the fee and consume the sequence; `tem`/`tef`/`ter`
leave the state identical.

## Model limitations

- Integer amounts; xrpld uses STAmount/Number with its own rounding.
- `ModelSwap` is one book step (single strand, exact-out, partial allowed). Multi-path Flow, strand ranking,
  auto-bridging, `LimitQuality`/`SendMax` and offer crossing via `OfferCreate` are not modeled.
- AMM is pool balances + fee + usable flag; LP tokens, `AMMDeposit`, `AMMBid` and `AMMClawback` are not modeled
  (`AMMWithdraw` all-out and issuer `TrustSet` freeze are modeled as subsets).
- Issuer transfer fees, MPT assets, Batch and delegation are specified but not modeled.
